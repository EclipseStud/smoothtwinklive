COMMON QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU GO.

How do I access the full experience?

Use any StripChat button to open the SmoothTwinkVibes profile in a new tab. StripChat shows the follow and live-chat controls available for your account and region. StripChat presents available account and purchase options after you arrive. Before leaving, review the three-step journey or see why following may fit.

Is this an 18+ environment?

Yes. This referral and its destination are intended only for adults aged 18 and over. Protected previews also require Clerk sign-in and a one-time, account-bound 18+ self-attestation. It is not ID-based age verification.

What if payment methods or access are restricted in my region?

This site does not process payments or collect payment details. StripChat controls prices, payment methods, and regional or account availability, which may vary. Check the information shown in your StripChat account or contact StripChat support.

What data does this page keep?

The current site code keeps up to 20 source, CTA, and mood events in sessionStorage for the current browser session and does not send that history to an external analytics endpoint. For protected previews, your Clerk user ID, attestation time, and policy version are stored server-side to remember access. StripChat applies its own privacy practices after you leave.

What accessibility support is included?

This page supports keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text, and reduced motion preferences. StripChat is a separate service with its own accessibility support.

What should I do if previews stay locked?

Return to the protected preview controls, sign in, complete the 18+ self-attestation, then use Retry. Access stays locked if Clerk or protected-storage configuration is unavailable. The public StripChat links remain available without opening protected previews.
