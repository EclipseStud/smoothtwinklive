How do I get to your live profile?

Use any pink StripChat button. It opens the exact SmoothTwinkVibes referral profile in a new tab. StripChat shows the profile and live-chat controls available for your account and region.

Is this an 18+ environment?

Yes. This site and its StripChat destination are intended only for adults aged 18 and over. The preview button is self-attestation, not identity or ID verification.

How does preview access work?

Press “I’m 18+ — show previews” once. A random browser token remembers the confirmation for 30 days. Only its SHA-256 hash, attestation time, expiration, and policy version are stored in D1—no account or identity is required.

What if payment methods or access vary in my region?

This site does not process payments or collect payment details. StripChat controls prices, payment methods, and regional or account availability. Check what StripChat shows after you arrive.

What data does this page keep?

Up to 20 source and CTA events stay in sessionStorage for this browser session and are not sent to an external analytics endpoint by this site. Preview confirmation uses the privacy-preserving token hash described above. StripChat has separate privacy practices after you leave.

What accessibility support is included?

Semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, descriptive image text, touch-friendly controls, and reduced motion support are included. StripChat is a separate service with its own accessibility support.

What if previews do not load?

Use the compact retry button in the preview section. The polished public placeholders stay visible, and every StripChat button keeps working even if preview storage or media is unavailable.
